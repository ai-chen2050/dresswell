Membership & Auto-Renewal Service Agreement

Effective date: 2026-10-29 · Applies to DressWell across all store channels

Welcome to DressWell Pro Membership. This Membership & Auto-Renewal Service Agreement ("Agreement") is an agreement between you and the developer of DressWell regarding your purchase and subscription to DressWell Pro services.

Please read and understand all terms carefully before subscribing. By checking the agreement box or completing a purchase, you acknowledge that you have read and agreed to this Agreement. If you do not agree, please do not purchase or subscribe.

1. Member Benefits

  • Unlimited 3D Virtual Try-On: No daily wear quotas, change and layer outfits freely;
  • Photograph Real Clothes for 3D Wardrobe: Digitize flat-lay garments into 3D items on your device;
  • Unlimited Lookbook Saves: Save and categorize looks for work, dates, and trips;
  • 17 Model Poses & 360° Runway: Dynamic poses and 360-degree rotation view;
  • 100% On-Device Privacy: Your body shape, facial parameters, and wardrobe never leave your device.

2. Subscription Types & Pricing

  • Monthly Subscription (Auto-Renewing): Billed monthly, auto-renews each month;
  • Yearly Subscription (Auto-Renewing): Billed annually at a discounted rate, auto-renews each year;
  • Lifetime Pro (One-Time Purchase): One-time fee, permanently valid on the same store account. No recurring fees, no auto-renewal;
  • Wear Pack (Consumable): 20 additional changes, non-recurring.

3. Auto-Renewal Rules

  • Billing Cycle: 1 month (Monthly) or 1 year (Yearly).
  • Auto-Charge Time: The app store billing system (Huawei AppGallery, Apple App Store, Google Play) will automatically charge your account within 24 hours prior to the end of the current billing cycle.
  • Renewal Extension: Upon successful payment, your Pro membership will automatically be extended for one billing cycle.
  • Failed Payment: If payment fails due to insufficient balance, the store will retry during a grace period; if payment still fails, your membership will expire at the end of the current cycle.

4. Cancellation / How to Cancel Auto-Renewal

You may cancel auto-renewal at any time. After cancellation, you can continue to enjoy Pro benefits until the end of the current paid period.

4.1 Huawei AppGallery

  • Device Settings Method: Open phone Settings → Tap your Huawei ID / Account Center at the top → Tap Payment and purchases → Tap Subscriptions / Auto-debit → Find DressWell (穿好衣) → Tap Cancel subscription.
  • AppGallery Method: Open AppGallery → Tap Me at the bottom right → Tap your profile avatar → Tap Payment and purchases → Tap Subscriptions → Find DressWell and cancel.

Note: To avoid being charged for the next period, please cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

4.2 Apple App Store (iOS)

Open iPhone Settings → Tap your Apple ID → Tap Subscriptions → Select DressWell → Tap Cancel Subscription.

4.3 Google Play (Android)

Open Google Play Store → Tap profile icon in the top right → Tap Payments & subscriptions → Tap Subscriptions → Select DressWell → Tap Cancel subscription.

5. Refunds & Policy Updates

  • Refunds: All payments and subscriptions are processed by the respective app stores. Refund requests must follow the refund policies of your app store platform.
  • Restore Purchases: If you switch devices, you can tap "Restore purchase" on the paywall screen at any time using your original store account.
