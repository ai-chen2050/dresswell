## 3. Features in detail

### 3.1 Real 3D you can spin, not a rendered picture
- **Summary**: A real-time 3D fitting room: turn to see the back, zoom into a cuff. Garment occlusion is computed geometry, not a guess.
- **Detail**: Photo-generation apps hand you one static image; a different angle means generating again, and when you layer, the dress under the coat is whatever the model imagines. DressWell renders in real time on the device (Google Filament). Coats, tops, bottoms, socks, shoes and hats each own a slot, and which garment tucks under which — plus every hidden face — is computed per vertex, so any angle and any combination stay consistent.

### 3.2 Your real proportions, not a flattering edit
- **Summary**: Height, body shape and facial features are a parametric human you shape yourself; garment fit is geometry.
- **Detail**: The usual photo-generation route has a familiar flaw: it makes you slimmer, taller, more standard — flattering, but not honest, and the garment still does not fit when it arrives. DressWell's avatar is a parametric human: height, shoulders, waist and hips, plus a dozen facial dials, all under your control. Clothes are fitted meshes rather than repaints, so the app does not flatter you — it tells you whether something actually fits. A photo can bring the avatar closer to you, and what it produces are starting values you can keep adjusting, not a fixed result.

### 3.3 Photograph a garment, and it joins your 3D wardrobe
- **Summary**: Lay a real garment flat, take one photo: background removed, colour and pattern recovered, projected front and back.
- **Detail**: The whole pipeline runs on the device: background removal, flattening the shading of wrinkles, white-balance correction, and mapping fabric along a shoulder-elbow-cuff path so sleeves follow a bent arm. Standard online-shop product shots — front-on, plain background, nobody wearing it — reconstruct best. The result layers and saves exactly like a built-in garment.

### 3.4 No servers, no account
- **Summary**: No cloud backend. Reconstruction, rendering and styling all happen locally; wardrobe and body data are never uploaded.
- **Detail**: No sign-up, no analytics, no ads. Your avatar, saved looks and uploaded garments live in the app's own sandbox, with optional backup into your personal iCloud container. The only case where anything leaves the device is if you configure your own model provider: the photo then goes straight to the endpoint you entered. We hold no key and proxy nothing, so we cannot see those requests either. Without a key the app works fully and every AI path falls back to manual input.

### 3.5 Scan your own bag into 3D (iPhone Pro)
- **Summary**: Circle a small object with a LiDAR iPhone, rebuild it on device, then hang it on the avatar's hand or head.
- **Detail**: Rebuilt on device with Apple's Object Capture; no photos are uploaded. A finished bag mounts to the hand joint, a hat to the head joint, with the hair and scalp it covers hidden automatically. This is the last mile from a digital wardrobe to a digital double, and it has no counterpart in photo-generation apps. Scanning is iOS-only, since Android lacks an equivalent on-device pipeline.

### 3.6 A stylist that runs on your own API key
- **Summary**: Optional AI styling advice: your provider, your bill. We do not resell inference.
- **Detail**: Enter your own model key in Settings — nine common providers come pre-filled with endpoints and model names — and the stylist suggests outfits by occasion, body shape and the wardrobe you actually own, remembering your preferences. It only recommends pieces you have. Skill packs are bundled prompt templates, not AI credit. Without a key the whole fitting room still works.

## 4. FAQ

### What is DressWell?
A 3D fitting room on your phone. You shape an avatar close to yourself — height, body, face, skin tone, hair — then style it with built-in garments or your own real clothes photographed into the app, and view it from any angle. Reconstruction, rendering and styling all run locally, and no account is required.

### Are my photos and body measurements uploaded?
Not by default. DressWell has no servers and no account system: your avatar, saved looks and uploaded garments live in the app's own sandbox, with optional backup into your personal iCloud container. The one exception is if you deliberately configure your own model provider in Settings — the photo then goes directly to the endpoint you entered and is handled under that provider's retention policy. We never hold your key and never proxy the request, so we cannot see it either.

### Does it work offline?
Yes. The fitting room, built-in garments, face and body shaping, and photographing your own clothes are all local computation and work in airplane mode. Only the optional AI styling advice needs a connection, because that step calls the model provider you configured yourself.

### How much does it cost?
Free to download and genuinely usable: all built-in garments, hairstyles and hats are open, body and face shaping are unlimited, outfit changes come with a free allowance of 20, and you can keep 2 saved looks. Pro is a one-time purchase that lasts forever and unlocks unlimited outfit changes, unlimited saved looks, and photographing your own clothes. The stylist's paid skill packs are priced separately and are not included in Pro. No subscription, no ads.

### Do I have to supply my own API key for the stylist?
Yes, and that is deliberate. You enter your own model provider in Settings — nine common ones come pre-filled — the request goes straight to that provider, and the bill is yours. That way we neither handle your photos nor need to fold inference costs into a subscription. Without a key the app still works, and everywhere AI would help falls back to doing it by hand.

### Which devices are supported?
iOS and Android. 3D rendering uses Google Filament and does ask something of the GPU, so frame rates drop on older phones. Scanning real objects with LiDAR is available only on iPhone Pro / Pro Max models that have it; Android does not offer that feature.

### How do I move my data to a new phone?
On iOS you can turn on iCloud backup; your avatar and custom garments sync into your own private iCloud container, and you restore them after installing the app on the new phone. If you bought Pro, tap Restore purchase on the paywall and sign in with the same store account — the entitlement comes back and you are not charged twice.

### How do I delete my data?
The privacy page in Settings lets you delete each item: saved looks, uploaded garments, and the photo used to shape the avatar. Deletion is local and immediate. Uninstalling the app removes everything in its sandbox. If you enabled iCloud backup, also remove the app's data in your system iCloud settings.
