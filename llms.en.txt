# DressWell (穿好衣, ai-dress)

> DressWell rebuilds your body and your real clothes as 3D you can spin: shape your own figure and face, photograph a garment to wear it, and layer coats, tops and dresses freely. Reconstruction, rendering and styling all run on the phone — your wardrobe and body data never leave the device.

## Overview

- **Name**: DressWell
- **Also known as**: 穿好衣, ai-dress
- **Positioning**: A 3D fitting room that lives on your phone
- **Platforms**: iOS / Android

## Core Features

1. **Real 3D you can spin, not a rendered picture**: A real-time 3D fitting room: turn to see the back, zoom into a cuff. Garment occlusion is computed geometry, not a guess.
2. **Your real proportions, not a flattering edit**: Height, body shape and facial features are a parametric human you shape yourself; garment fit is geometry.
3. **Photograph a garment, and it joins your 3D wardrobe**: Lay a real garment flat, take one photo: background removed, colour and pattern recovered, projected front and back.
4. **No servers, no account**: No cloud backend. Reconstruction, rendering and styling all happen locally; wardrobe and body data are never uploaded.
5. **Scan your own bag into 3D (iPhone Pro)**: Circle a small object with a LiDAR iPhone, rebuild it on device, then hang it on the avatar's hand or head.
6. **A stylist that runs on your own API key**: Optional AI styling advice: your provider, your bill. We do not resell inference.
